OUR MISSION

Technology should create opportunity.

Neural Chassis exists to make reliable computing more accessible while giving unused technology a second life.

WHY WE EXIST

Two problems. One solution.

Access to technology can shape someone's ability to learn, work, communicate, and build.

Yet usable computers and components are often replaced long before their useful life is over.

Neural Chassis works to close that gap by recovering hardware, restoring it, and putting complete systems where they can create meaningful opportunities.

Our belief

Access to technology should be a starting point.
