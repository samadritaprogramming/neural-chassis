01 / THE PROBLEM

Technology shouldn't be a barrier.

Reliable access to a computer can shape how someone learns, works, creates, and connects with the world.

At the same time, perfectly usable hardware is constantly replaced, discarded, or left sitting unused.

Neural Chassis connects those two problems. We recover usable technology, give it a second life, and help place it where it can make a difference.

What gets left behind

can become what comes next.
